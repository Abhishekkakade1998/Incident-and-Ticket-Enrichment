Hot Feedwater Equipment Isolation Safety Instruction

Hazards

Boiler feedwater systems operate at high temperature and pressure. Leaking water flashes to steam at atmospheric pressure and causes scalding. Pumps that have been isolated may remain hot and pressurised for several hours.

Isolation Requirements

  1. Obtain a permit to work signed by the area authority.
  2. Apply double-block-and-bleed isolation on suction and discharge.
  3. Isolate and lock out the motor at the MCC and verify zero energy with a start attempt.
  4. Drain and depressurise the pump casing to the closed drain system and confirm zero pressure on the local gauge.
  5. Allow the casing to cool below 60 degC before breaking any joint.

Personal Protective Equipment

Face shield, thermal gloves and flame-resistant clothing are mandatory when approaching a leaking mechanical seal on a hot feedwater pump.
